A NIGHT OF LANTERNS

Our Story

Every October the falls of Hollowmere run pale. For one night the canyon lights its lanterns, the old houses open their doors, and the ghosts come home to visit.

We have kept the night for two hundred years: a walk through the crypts, the mill and the cauldron halls, ending at the lake where the lanterns are set free.

The Rites

The Lantern Walk.

At dusk the procession leaves the castle gate and follows the ghost-light falls down into the canyon. Bring a lantern, or borrow one of ours at the gate.

Children lead, the old walk last, and nobody looks back until the bells stop.

The Turning Wheel.

Below the falls the old mill turns all night. Write a wish on a paper lantern, pin it to a spoke, and let the wheel carry it round to midnight.

Plan Your Night

Gates at Six.

The gates open at 18:00 on 31 October. The walk takes about ninety minutes, and the cauldron halls stay open until three, serving spiced cider, soul cakes and hot pumpkin broth.

Midnight at the Cauldron.

At the twelfth bell the great cauldron is lit and every lantern in Hollowmere is carried down to the lake. Stay for it.
